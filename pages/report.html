Introduction

Electronic Arts (EA) who is a renowned global company that leads in the realm of video game development and interactive entertainment technology to over 600 million players. This company specializes in creating captivating gaming experiences across various platforms, encompassing areas of computing science such as graphics rendering, artificial intelligence, physics simulations, and network programming. Through their innovative work, EA merges technology and creativity to craft the immersive games enjoyed by millions worldwide. Notably, EA is responsible for developing popular gaming franchises like FC (formally FIFA), Madden NFL, The Sims, and more, impacting the entertainment industry positively.

Job Description

For my 8-month internship at Electronic Arts (EA), I worked as a Software Engineer on the Client Team for the new FC24 game dealing with both front-end and back-end features for the game. My main responsibility up to this point in my internship is related to bug fixes within FUT, so this includes finding them, fixing them and testing scenarios which may lead to a bug. Since I work in the UT mode, there are a lot of sub-branched game modes like clubs, squad battles, evolutions, etc meaning that the bugs I tackle are vastly different in specific scenarios. But bug fixing is the only thing I do, I have also dealt with crash dump reports. These are essentially errors in the code base which cause the game to crash while performing a specific task playing the game. So my job was to identify the point of error and check if it is a problem on our team’s end or if it is from a different team and if so I have to do the appropriate tasks to inform them. And lastly my other tasks were to re-design specific screens and add additional features and functionalities to the game.

Goals

The general goals I set for this work term revolved around Teamwork Skills, Problem Skills and Communication Skills. But I have also set goals that were directly related to my job tasks, some of which include learning the companies workflow, learning company related software, and connecting with all my team members.

  • Goal 1: Professional & Ethical Behaviour – Teamwork
    • My primary objective as an intern at EA is to enhance my teamwork skills within a professional environment. At EA, teamwork is highly valued, and there is a strong emphasis on collaboration across all teams in the company. By working with a team of experts in this area, I am eager to gain new insights and ideas to further develop my teamwork abilities. I also have the ability to expand my teamwork skills by working with people from different discipline areas such as the server team and expand my network. I am excited to build upon my existing skills and strive for continuous improvement
    • Reflection:
      • Now that I have successfully completed my first work term with EA, I feel that I have achieved this goal of “Teamwork” quite well. I was able to establish meaningful connections with a diverse range of team members. This enabled me to engage with not only Software Engineers but also designers, producers and even server engineers which in the end helped facilitate the successful completion of my tasks/duties. Additionally, I have extended my networking efforts beyond my immediate team, actively participating in social company events, such as “Interns Talk with CEO”, “Future at EA” and “Inter Fair”. These interactions have provided valuable insights into the diverse projects undertaken within the company and my potential future with them.
  • Goal 2: Critical & Creative Thinking – Problem Solving
    • Problem-solving is an essential skill in my role as a software engineer here at EA. Given the nature of my work, I anticipate encountering numerous challenges that require efficient resolution. Therefore, my objective is to continuously develop my problem-solving abilities to a level where I can confidently and effectively address any issue that comes my way. By honing my problem-solving skills, I aim to enhance my capacity to tackle complex problems and find optimal solutions within the context of my role as a software engineer at EA.
    • Reflection:
      • During the initial phase of my internship, I believe I've made substantial progress toward this objective. As a Software Engineer on the team, I was able to solve multiple bugs that were assigned to me and was slowly able to increase on the complexity of the tasks as well. Starting with simple front-end bugs that dealt with screen fonts and images, I am now able to work on backend bugs which deal with data that builds the foundation of the FC game itself. As of now, I was able to complete just over 30 bugs for the game. Through these bug-fixing tasks, I've cultivated a range of problem-solving skills. These encompass navigating through an extensive codebase to locate specific code segments, exploring diverse approaches to issue resolution, determining reliable resources for seeking assistance in bug resolution, and more. These newfound problem-solving proficiencies have significantly deepened my comprehension of task resolution within the industry, furnishing me with valuable readiness for future challenges.
  • Goal 3: Communicating – Oral Communication
    • As an intern communication is key, everything is new and is a learning experience. I will have a lot of questions during my term, and the most effective approach to address these questions is through clear and proactive communication with the appropriate individuals.
    • Reflection:
      • After the first four months of my internship, I have already experienced substantial progress in pursuit of this goal. Initially, initiating conversations with various team members posed a challenge for me, primarily due to uncertainty about whom to approach with specific inquiries. However, through interactions with my manager and technical lead over time, I've gained insights into the distinct roles each team member plays. This has enabled me to discern the appropriate individuals to seek assistance from and when to do so. The consistent presence of team members on Slack greatly facilitated this process. Utilizing dedicated channels tailored to specific topics, I maximized my engagement with discussions. Additionally, I successfully established bi-weekly meetings with my manager, ensuring our alignment and maintaining transparency regarding my ongoing tasks. This practice has proven instrumental in keeping both of us well-informed.
