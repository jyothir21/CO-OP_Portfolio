Employer Information

Electronic Arts (EA) is a renowned global company that leads in the realm of video game development and interactive entertainment technology to over 600 million players. This company specializes in creating captivating gaming experiences across various platforms, encompassing areas of computer science such as graphics rendering, artificial intelligence, physics simulations, and network programming. Through their innovative work, EA merges technology and creativity to craft the immersive games enjoyed by millions worldwide. Notably, EA is responsible for developing popular gaming franchises like FC (formally FIFA), Madden, NFL, The Sims, and more, impacting the entertainment industry positively.

Job Description

During the first four months of my internship at Electronic Arts (EA), I served as a C++ Software Engineer on the Client Team for the new FC24 game, working on both front-end and back-end features. My primary responsibility during this phase of my internship was centered around bug fixing within the Ultimate Team (UT) game mode. This task involved the identification, testing, and resolution of various bugs, each presenting unique challenges and valuable learning experiences. I worked extensively with C++, ActionScript, and XML in this capacity. As I became more immersed in the role of a Software Engineer, my responsibilities expanded to include redesigning aspects of the game, introducing new features and functionalities, and managing crash dump reports, which I found particularly intriguing. These reports represented errors in the codebase that led to game crashes during specific in-game actions. My role entailed identifying the source of the error and determining whether it originated within our team or elsewhere, necessitating appropriate communication and action. This experience greatly enhanced both my technical and interpersonal skills, as it involved a well-defined sequence of tasks. These experiences were like a big step forward in my learning as they provided insights into the company's workflow and the entire process of implementing changes into the mainstream game.

Most of the skills needed for the job were learned during onboarding. This included becoming familiar with company software like Perforce, Jira, Slack, and Frostbite Drone, as well as participating in stand-up meetings and understanding the bug ticketing process. However, one critical skill I could directly apply from my school background was C++ programming. In the first two years of my education, we extensively covered C and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) concepts, which made it relatively easy to transition to a programming language I had previously learned. Consequently, I was able to quickly enhance my proficiency in C++. Moreover, I leveraged this knowledge to pick up a new programming language, ActionScript, which was used by the company and proved to be highly beneficial.

Goals

The general goals I set for this work term revolved around building teamwork, problem-solving, and communication skills. However, I also established goals directly related to my tasks. Some of these tasks include learning the company's workflow, becoming proficient in company-related software, and building connections with all my team members.

  • Goal 1: Professional & Ethical Behaviour – Teamwork
    • My primary objective as an intern at EA is to enhance my teamwork skills within a professional environment. At EA, teamwork is highly valued, and there is a strong emphasis on collaboration across all teams in the company. By working with a team of experts in this area, I am eager to gain new insights and ideas to further develop my teamwork abilities. Additionally, I have the opportunity to expand my teamwork skills by collaborating with individuals from different disciplines, such as the server team, which will help me broaden my network. I am excited to build upon my existing skills and strive for continuous improvement.

    • Reflection:
      • Now that I have successfully completed my first work term with EA, I feel that I have achieved the goal of 'Teamwork' quite effectively. I was able to establish meaningful connections with a diverse range of team members, enabling me to engage not only with Software Engineers but also with designers, producers, and even server engineers. These connections were instrumental in facilitating the successful completion of my tasks and duties.
      • Furthermore, I extended my networking efforts beyond my immediate team, actively participating in social company events such as 'Interns Talk with CEO,' 'Future at EA,' and 'Inter Fair.' These interactions provided valuable insights into the diverse projects undertaken within the company and my potential future with them.

  • Goal 2: Critical & Creative Thinking – Problem Solving
    • Problem-solving is an essential skill in my role as a software engineer here at EA. Given the nature of my work, I anticipate encountering numerous challenges that will require efficient resolution. Therefore, my objective is to continuously develop my problem-solving abilities to a level where I can confidently and effectively address any issue that arises. By honing my problem-solving skills, I aim to enhance my capacity to tackle complex problems and find optimal solutions within the context of my role as a software engineer at EA.

    • Reflection:
      • During the initial phase of my internship, I believe I've made substantial progress toward this objective. As a Software Engineer on the team, I successfully resolved multiple assigned bugs and gradually took on more complex tasks. I started with straightforward front-end bugs related to screen fonts and images and have now progressed to tackling back-end bugs that pertain to the core data of the FC game itself. So far, I've completed over 30 bugs for the game.
      • Through these bug-fixing tasks, I've developed a wide range of problem-solving skills, including navigating an extensive codebase to locate specific code segments, exploring various approaches to issue resolution, and identifying reliable resources for seeking assistance in bug resolution. These newly acquired problem-solving skills have significantly deepened my understanding of task resolution within the industry, providing me with required confidence for facing future challenges.

  • Goal 3: Communicating – Oral Communication
    • As an intern, communication is key, as everything is new and a learning experience. I anticipate having many questions during my term, and the most effective approach to addressing these questions is through clear and proactive communication with the appropriate individuals.

    • Reflection:
      • After the first four months of my internship, I have already made substantial progress in pursuit of this goal. Initially, initiating conversations with various team members posed a challenge for me, primarily due to uncertainty about whom to approach with specific inquiries. However, through interactions with my manager and technical lead over time, I've gained insights into the distinct roles each team member plays. This has enabled me to discern the appropriate individuals to seek assistance from and when to do so. The consistent presence of team members on Slack greatly facilitated this process. By utilizing dedicated channels tailored to specific topics, I maximized my engagement with discussions. Additionally, I successfully established bi-weekly meetings with my manager, ensuring our alignment and maintaining transparency regarding my ongoing tasks. This practice has proven instrumental in keeping both of us well-informed.
      • Furthermore, my regular visits to the local office here in Toronto have been a significant game-changer for me. They provided me with valuable firsthand experience in an office setting, allowing me the opportunity to network and personally interact with team members. It was an exceptionally enjoyable and enriching experience.

This has been my first co-op experience, and I must say it's been a fantastic opportunity to get a glimpse of the kind of work I might be doing in the future. Working at Electronic Arts (EA) has been a real learning experience. They've not only helped me improve my coding skills in languages I already knew but also allowed me to work with new frameworks, technologies, and languages. Additionally, I've grown as a team player during this experience. I've had the opportunity to effectively share my ideas and collaborate closely with my teammates. I'm genuinely thankful for the chance to acquire and practice new skills, which will undoubtedly enhance my qualifications for future job opportunities.
